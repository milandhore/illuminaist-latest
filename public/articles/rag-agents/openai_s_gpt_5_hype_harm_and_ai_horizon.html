The Evolution of AI: A Leap from GPT-4 to GPT-5

While GPT-4 was a game-changer, GPT-5 is in a league of its own. Here are some of the key improvements that set it apart:

  • Enhanced Reasoning and Accuracy: GPT-5 is significantly better at understanding complex queries and providing accurate, nuanced responses. It’s less prone to making up facts and can follow intricate instructions with greater precision.
  • Superior Coding Capabilities: For developers, GPT-5 is a dream come true. It can write, debug, and explain code with a level of proficiency that surpasses any previous model. It can even generate entire applications from a single prompt.
  • Advanced Multimodality: GPT-5 is not limited to text. It can understand and generate images, audio, and video with remarkable fluency. This opens up a world of possibilities for creative and interactive applications.
  • Agentic Capabilities: One of the most exciting advancements in GPT-5 is its ability to act as an agent. It can perform multi-step tasks, use tools, and even browse the web to find information. This makes it a powerful tool for automation and problem-solving.

Technical Evaluation: GPT-5 by the Numbers

While the qualitative improvements are impressive, a look at the technical benchmarks reveals just how significant the leap to GPT-5 is. The model sets a new state-of-the-art across multiple domains, from complex coding to advanced mathematical reasoning.

Coding and Development Expertise

In the critical domain of software development, GPT-5 establishes a new standard. On SWE-bench , which tests a model’s ability to solve real-world GitHub issues, it achieves a 74.9% success rate. On Aider Polyglot , a benchmark for multi-language code editing, GPT-5 scores a remarkable 88% , representing a one-third reduction in error rate compared to the already capable o3 model.

Mathematical and Scientific Reasoning

The model’s logical reasoning extends to advanced mathematics and science. On the AIME 2025 competition-level math problems, GPT-5 scores 94.6% without tools, demonstrating a powerful grasp of complex mathematical concepts. This superior reasoning makes it a more reliable partner for technical and scientific problem-solving.

Reliability and Reduced Hallucinations

Beyond raw performance, GPT-5 is engineered for greater reliability. Internal evaluations show it is approximately 45% less likely to produce factual errors than GPT-4o. This focus on accuracy makes it a more trustworthy tool for high-stakes applications in fields like healthcare and finance.

Under the Hood: Technical Advancements in GPT-5

So, what’s the secret sauce behind GPT-5’s impressive capabilities? It’s been trained on a massive dataset and uses a new, more efficient architecture. One of the key technical innovations is its use of mixture-of-experts (MoE) , which allows the model to activate only the relevant parts of its neural network for a given task.

For developers, GPT-5 offers unprecedented control through the API. It comes in three sizes ( gpt-5 , gpt-5-mini , and gpt-5-nano ) and introduces new parameters to fine-tune performance:

  • reasoning_effort : Allows developers to balance response speed and analytical depth.
  • verbosity : Provides direct control over the length and detail of the model's output.
