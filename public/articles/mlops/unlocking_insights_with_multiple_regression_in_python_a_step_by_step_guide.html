Introduction

Predictive modeling is a method of predicting future outcomes by using data modeling. It’s one of the premier ways a business can see its path forward and make plans accordingly. While not foolproof, this method tends to have high accuracy rates, which is why it is so commonly used. In short, predictive modeling is a statistical technique using machine learning and data mining to predict and forecast likely future outcomes with the aid of historical and existing data. It works by analyzing current and historical data and projecting what it learns on a model generated to forecast likely outcomes.

A1: Research Question

Can we predict from the available list of variables that how many GBs of data are used by customers yearly? We also need to find out the independent variable.

A2: Objectives and Goal

Customer churn rate is one of the critical key performance indicators (KPI) for the subscription business model. For this performance assessment and analysis, we would like to understand the amount of data in GBs used by customers yearly. The significant objective of this analysis is to identify high to low internet data plan users which indeed helps an organization to offer relevant services and discounts to such customers.

Customers who are using very low internet data services then such customer might be at higher risk of churn or could be unsatisfied with services. Predication of such customers helps the organization to offer them discounted or relevant data plans with 1–3 years contracts for customer retention. For the unsatisfied customer, the organization can collect the subjective survey in detail. With the help of such kind of analysis, we can create a business value case for the organization and promote discounted data plans and contracts to low GBs data users. Retaining customers with the service provider is equally important as gaining new customers. For customers with more internet data usages, the organization can offer more discounted prices to retain such customers with the service provider.
