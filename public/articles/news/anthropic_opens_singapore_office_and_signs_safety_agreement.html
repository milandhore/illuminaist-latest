Anthropic opens Singapore office and signs safety agreement

Anthropic is expanding in Singapore and will work with the government on AI safety, security, skills, and responsible adoption.

Anthropic has opened a Singapore office and signed a memorandum with Singapore’s Ministry of Digital Development and Information. The partnership will focus on AI safety, security, skills, and responsible adoption.

The expansion reinforces Singapore’s role as an AI hub and shows how frontier labs are combining commercial growth with government-facing safety work.
