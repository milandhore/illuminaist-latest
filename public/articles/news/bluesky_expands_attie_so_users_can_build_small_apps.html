Bluesky expands Attie so users can build small apps

Bluesky’s AI tool can now turn prompts into games, quizzes, websites, and other shareable experiences hosted on the AT Protocol.

Bluesky has expanded its experimental Attie platform from AI-generated feeds to small websites, games, quizzes, and other interactive experiences. The outputs can be shared through the decentralized AT Protocol.

Prompt-to-app tools could broaden participation in software creation, but publishing, moderation, security, and maintenance still need to be handled beyond the initial generation step.
