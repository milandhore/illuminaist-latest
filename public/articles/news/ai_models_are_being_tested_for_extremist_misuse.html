AI models are being tested for extremist misuse

A new report warns that open-weight models can be modified to remove safeguards and support dangerous activity.

Researchers studying open-weight models found that safeguards can sometimes be stripped or weakened with relatively little effort. The findings raise concerns about how easily capable systems can be repurposed for extremist or violent activity.

The report adds pressure for better release evaluations, abuse monitoring, and support for defenders without eliminating the research and customization benefits of open models.
