Wikipedia tightens rules around AI-generated content

Wikipedia editors are restricting AI-generated writing beyond translation and proofreading to protect editorial standards.

Wikipedia editors are tightening rules around AI-generated content after finding machine-written material in new articles. The debate centers on whether AI can support maintenance without weakening human review and editorial accountability.

The decision is a useful case study for any organization balancing automated drafting with a high-trust knowledge base.
