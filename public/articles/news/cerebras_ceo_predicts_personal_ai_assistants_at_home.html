Cerebras CEO predicts personal AI assistants at home

Cerebras says local chips and cloud systems could soon make personal AI assistants as common as other home appliances.

Cerebras CEO Andrew Feldman predicts that personal AI assistants will become common household devices, combining local processing with cloud services for tasks such as scheduling and payments.

The vision depends on affordable inference, dependable voice interfaces, and controls that keep sensitive actions reviewable before an assistant commits them.
