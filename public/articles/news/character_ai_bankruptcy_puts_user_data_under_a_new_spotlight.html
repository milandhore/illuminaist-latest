Character AI bankruptcy puts user data under a new spotlight

State regulators are asking how chatbot user data will be protected as Character AI’s assets move through bankruptcy proceedings.

Character AI’s bankruptcy proceedings have prompted regulators to question how personal conversations and account data will be handled if the company’s assets are sold. The case shows how data protection obligations can persist through a corporate transition.

AI services should make data portability, deletion, retention, and acquisition scenarios explicit before a crisis forces those questions into court.
