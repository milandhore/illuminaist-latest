Pluto Security raises funding for enterprise AI controls

Pluto Security is building real-time monitoring and policy enforcement for AI tools used across enterprise teams.

Pluto Security has raised funding to monitor AI tools and agents used by enterprise teams. Its platform aims to detect risky behavior and enforce policies without blocking legitimate work.

As AI spreads into finance, marketing, and legal workflows, security teams need visibility into permissions, data movement, tool calls, and the difference between an assistant and an autonomous actor.
