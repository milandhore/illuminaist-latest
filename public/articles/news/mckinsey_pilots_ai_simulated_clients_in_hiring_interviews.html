McKinsey pilots AI-simulated clients in hiring interviews

McKinsey is testing a short interview exercise where candidates interact with an AI-simulated client before human evaluation.

McKinsey plans to pilot an interview stage in which candidates hold a short conversation with an AI-simulated client. Human interviewers will still assess the performance, using the simulation to create a more realistic consulting scenario.

The experiment raises familiar questions about evaluation fairness, transparency, and whether candidates should be judged on their interaction with a model.
