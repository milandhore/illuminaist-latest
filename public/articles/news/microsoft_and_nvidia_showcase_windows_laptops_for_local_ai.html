Microsoft and NVIDIA showcase Windows laptops for local AI

New RTX Spark laptops combine Arm processors and powerful GPUs to accelerate AI workloads on Windows devices.

Microsoft and NVIDIA showcased RTX Spark laptops designed around Arm processors and high-end GPUs for AI workloads. The platform is aimed at faster local model execution and other compute-heavy tasks.

The push could make private, low-latency inference more practical on developer machines, though application compatibility and device pricing remain important constraints.
