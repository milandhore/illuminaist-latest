AI-powered organ preservation moves closer to transplant use

Scientists restored function in pig kidneys stored at extremely low temperatures for a year, pointing toward longer-term organ banking.

Scientists vitrified pig kidneys at about -150°C for up to a year, rewarmed them, and recovered function after transplantation into pigs. The result suggests that organs might eventually be stored for much longer than current methods allow.

The research is not yet a human treatment, but it could help address transplant shortages if the preservation process can be scaled safely and consistently.

Source: MIT Technology Review
