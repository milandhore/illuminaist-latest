Eight states sue OpenAI over chatbot safety

State lawsuits accuse OpenAI of consumer-protection violations and allege that chatbot safeguards failed vulnerable users.

Eight US states have filed lawsuits alleging that OpenAI’s chatbot caused harm to children and other vulnerable users and that the company did not adequately warn about safety risks. The cases put model behavior and product claims under legal scrutiny.

The litigation could influence how AI companies document safeguards, assess foreseeable misuse, and communicate limitations to customers.
