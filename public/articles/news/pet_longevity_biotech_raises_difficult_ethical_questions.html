Pet-longevity biotech raises difficult ethical questions

Companies are offering drugs, cloning, and cryonics for pets, while experts warn that AI-enabled biotech can amplify unrealistic expectations.

Biotech companies are offering medicines, cloning, and cryonics aimed at extending pets’ lives. The services combine advances in biology and data-driven research with a market built around owners’ fear of loss.

The trend raises familiar questions for AI-enabled biotech: what evidence supports a claim, how should risks be communicated, and where does compassionate care end and commercial overpromising begin?
