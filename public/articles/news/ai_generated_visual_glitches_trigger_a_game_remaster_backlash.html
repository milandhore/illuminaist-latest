AI-generated visual glitches trigger a game-remaster backlash

Sega pulled a remaster after players criticized bugs and AI-generated visual problems, underscoring the cost of rushed AI upgrades.

Sega withdrew a remaster after a troubled launch that included bugs and AI-generated visual glitches. The response shows how quickly audiences can reject AI-assisted production when the result appears unfinished or misleading.

For creative teams, transparent labeling and human quality control remain essential when generative tools are used in commercial releases.
