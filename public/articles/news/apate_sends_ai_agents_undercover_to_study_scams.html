Apate sends AI agents undercover to study scams

An Australian startup uses conversational agents to engage scammers and collect intelligence for banks, telcos, and governments.

Australian startup Apate.ai deploys AI agents that pose as scam victims, engage fraudsters, and collect information such as bank details and crypto wallets. The conversations are used to understand campaigns and help organizations respond earlier.

The approach shows how agents can be used defensively, but it also requires careful limits around impersonation, evidence handling, and escalation to law enforcement.
