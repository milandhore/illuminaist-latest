Pig kidneys preserved for a year revived after transplant

Scientists restored function in pig kidneys kept vitrified for a year, pointing toward longer-term organ banking.

Scientists have vitrified pig kidneys at extremely low temperatures for up to a year, rewarmed them, and restored function after transplantation into pigs. The work suggests that long-term organ banking may become more practical.

Human trials are still ahead, but the result could eventually make transplant logistics less dependent on a narrow window between donation and surgery.

Source: MIT Technology Review
