Google and Singapore put scam detection on-device

A new DeepMind and GovTech model analyzes scam conversations on phones, warning users without sending private messages to the cloud.

Google DeepMind and Singapore’s GovTech have introduced an on-device scam-detection model trained on local fraud conversations. It aims to warn users in real time while keeping private messages off remote servers.

On-device detection could improve privacy and latency, but teams will still need to measure false positives, model updates, and how well the system handles new scam patterns.
