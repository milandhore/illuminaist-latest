Xbox explores AI-powered game ports and modding

Microsoft is creating an Xbox Labs team to explore AI-assisted decompilation, modding, and ports of classic games.

Microsoft is launching an Xbox Labs team to investigate AI-assisted game decompilation, modding, and ports. The work could help fans remix older titles and make legacy software easier to adapt.

The effort will need to navigate copyright, security, quality assurance, and the difference between preserving a game and redistributing protected code or assets.
