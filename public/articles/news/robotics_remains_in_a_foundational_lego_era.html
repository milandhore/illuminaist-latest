Robotics remains in a foundational ‘Lego era’

A former NVIDIA robotics leader says physical AI still needs better components, standards, and integration before a ChatGPT moment.

Amulya Vishwanath, a former NVIDIA robotics product leader, says physical AI is still rebuilding its basic components and standards. She compares the sector to a Lego era rather than a field ready for one foundation model to unlock everything.

The view is a reminder that robotics progress depends as much on sensors, materials, control systems, and deployment economics as on model capability.
