Liquid AI brings private agents to smaller local hardware

LiquidAI’s compact mixture-of-experts model is designed for fast, private, tool-using agents on local devices.

LiquidAI unveiled LFM2.5-24B-A2B, a mixture-of-experts model that activates only a small portion of its parameters for each token. The design targets fast, private agents that can reason, use tools, and complete multi-step tasks on local hardware.

Efficient models can make agentic software less dependent on cloud APIs, particularly where latency, data residency, or offline operation matter.
