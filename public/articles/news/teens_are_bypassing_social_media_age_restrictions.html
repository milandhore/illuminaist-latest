Teens are bypassing social-media age restrictions

Recent studies suggest many minors can evade age checks, raising doubts about blunt bans and the role of AI in safer online design.

Research and reporting from several countries indicate that many teenagers can bypass new social-media age restrictions. Critics argue that blunt bans may push young users toward less visible and less safe parts of the internet.

The policy challenge is shifting toward better age assurance, safer defaults, and platform accountability rather than relying on a single automated gate.

Source: MIT Technology Review
