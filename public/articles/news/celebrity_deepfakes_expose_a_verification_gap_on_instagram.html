Celebrity deepfakes expose a verification gap on Instagram

AI-generated celebrity accounts with fake verification marks are spreading, showing how synthetic media can borrow trust from real identities.

AI-generated videos and images featuring celebrities are spreading through Instagram, sometimes using fake accounts and verification signals to appear credible. The content can exploit major events and popular music to gain reach.

Platforms need stronger identity provenance, faster reporting paths, and better user education as synthetic media becomes harder to distinguish from authentic posts.
