Google DeepMind launches an on-device scam detector in Singapore

Google DeepMind and Singapore’s GovTech introduced an on-device model that warns about scams without uploading private messages.

Google DeepMind and Singapore’s GovTech have launched an on-device scam-detection model trained on local fraud conversations. The system is designed to warn users in real time while keeping private messages on the device.

On-device inference can reduce data exposure, but it also shifts responsibility toward efficient models, safe update mechanisms, and clear explanations for why a message was flagged.
