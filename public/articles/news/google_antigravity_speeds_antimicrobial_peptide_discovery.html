Google Antigravity speeds antimicrobial-peptide discovery

Google DeepMind’s workbench uses AI to scan proteins for antimicrobial candidates and has identified a peptide moving toward lab validation.

Google DeepMind’s Antigravity workbench uses the ProtNLM model to scan millions of uncharacterized proteins for antimicrobial peptide candidates. One candidate targeting respiratory infections is moving toward laboratory validation.

The project shows AI’s value as a search and prioritization layer for biology, while experimental validation remains essential before any clinical claim can be made.
