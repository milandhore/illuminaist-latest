Google Play lets users build private vibe-coded Android apps

Google AI Studio can now generate private apps that run on a user’s Android device without publishing them publicly.

Google is allowing users to create private AI-generated Android apps through Google AI Studio and run them on their own devices. The apps can remain unlisted while Google develops options for controlled sharing.

Private app generation lowers the barrier to prototyping, but secure permissions, dependency review, and safe distribution will matter as non-developers generate executable software.
