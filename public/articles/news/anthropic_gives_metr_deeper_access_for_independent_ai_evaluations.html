Anthropic gives METR deeper access for independent AI evaluations

Anthropic is giving outside evaluators employee-level access to internal tools and models to strengthen frontier AI oversight.

Anthropic has granted researchers from the nonprofit METR access to internal tools and model information for independent evaluations of safety and risk management. The arrangement is intended to let evaluators publish findings without a company veto.

The experiment offers a more demanding model for third-party oversight as frontier labs face pressure to make their safety claims auditable.
