China proposes tighter export controls for AI technologies

China has proposed restrictions covering 23 AI technologies as it frames advanced models and agents as national-security concerns.

China has proposed restricting or banning exports of 40 technologies spanning AI, chips, and solar power. The draft list includes 23 AI technologies, from advanced large models to agents, and cites national security.

The proposal shows how AI policy is becoming part of technology trade strategy, with export controls likely to shape model access, hardware supply, and international partnerships.

Source: Associated Press
