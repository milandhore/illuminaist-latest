AI-designed proteins cut other proteins with precision

Researchers used generative protein design to create enzymes that target specific proteins linked to neurological disorders.

Scientists used AI-driven protein design to rework protease enzymes so they could target selected proteins. Lab tests showed that the engineered proteins could cleave targets associated with neurological disease.

The work demonstrates how generative models can move from predicting biological structures to designing functional molecules, while still requiring extensive laboratory validation and safety review.
