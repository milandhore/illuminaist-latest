TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 closes with AI and robotics in focus

The conference highlighted AI infrastructure, robotics, security, and startups competing to turn emerging technology into products.

TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 closed with demonstrations and discussions spanning AI hardware, robotics, health technology, and security. The event showed how quickly frontier model work is being connected to physical products and operational systems.

The recurring theme was execution: dependable infrastructure, distribution, and safety practices are becoming as important as model novelty.
