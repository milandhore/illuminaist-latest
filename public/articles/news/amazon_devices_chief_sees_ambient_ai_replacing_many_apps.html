Amazon devices chief sees ambient AI replacing many apps

Amazon is shifting toward ambient AI devices that anticipate tasks instead of making people open a separate app for every request.

Amazon devices chief Panos Panay described a future in which ambient AI devices operate in the background and reduce the need to navigate traditional apps. New wearables are designed to capture context and turn it into useful actions.

The strategy puts privacy architecture at the center: encrypted transcripts, deletion of raw audio, and transparent controls will determine whether ambient assistants earn trust.
