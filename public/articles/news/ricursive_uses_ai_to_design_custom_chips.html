Ricursive uses AI to design custom chips

Startup Ricursive says its AI can automate parts of semiconductor layout and iteration as demand for compute continues to rise.

Ricursive Intelligence says its AI system can help design and iterate semiconductor layouts, including unconventional shapes aimed at efficiency. The startup is targeting applications from data centers to space and augmented reality.

AI-assisted chip design could shorten development cycles, but production quality, verification, and fabrication constraints remain the real test of the approach.
