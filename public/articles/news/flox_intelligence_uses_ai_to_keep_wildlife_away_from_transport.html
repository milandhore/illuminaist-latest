Flox Intelligence uses AI to keep wildlife away from transport

Flox combines edge AI and species-specific deterrents to reduce wildlife collisions near trains, airports, and roads.

Flox Intelligence has developed edge devices that detect wildlife near transport infrastructure and emit species-specific deterrent sounds. The system adapts over time to reduce habituation and has been deployed at multiple sites.

The project shows how narrow, continuously learning systems can address physical-world problems without requiring a general-purpose assistant.
