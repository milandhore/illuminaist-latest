AI weather emulator targets extreme events at lower cost

HiRO-ACEv2 generates high-resolution weather data and rare-event simulations at a fraction of the cost of traditional models.

Researchers introduced HiRO-ACEv2, an AI climate emulator that generates global weather data at roughly 3-kilometer resolution. The system is designed to model rare rain, wind, and pressure events more cheaply than conventional high-resolution simulations.

Lower simulation costs could expand climate research and planning, especially if the model’s extreme-event statistics continue to hold up against observations.
