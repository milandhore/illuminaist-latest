IBM Granite Switch 4.1 unifies chat, decisions, and speech

IBM’s new model family combines conversation, decision support, and speech-to-text through modular adapters on one endpoint.

IBM has released Granite Switch 4.1, a model family that combines chat, decision-making, and speech-to-text through modular adapters. The 8B and 30B variants include decision and safety adapters plus a speech model.

A modular approach could help teams reuse one serving layer across several workloads while tuning behavior for domain-specific safety and performance requirements.
