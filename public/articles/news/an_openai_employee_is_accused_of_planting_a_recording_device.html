An OpenAI employee is accused of planting a recording device

A case involving an alleged hidden recorder highlights insider-risk concerns around corporate AI tools and sensitive workspaces.

An OpenAI employee has been accused of using an internal chatbot while planning to place a covert recording device in a company conference room. The case has raised questions about insider threats and misuse of workplace AI tools.

Organizations deploying internal assistants need strong access boundaries, security monitoring, and policies that account for the model itself as a potential source of operational guidance.
