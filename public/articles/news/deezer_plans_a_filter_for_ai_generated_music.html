Deezer plans a filter for AI-generated music

Deezer says users will be able to hide AI-generated songs, albums, and artists from search results later this year.

Deezer plans to add a setting that lets listeners filter AI-generated music from search results. The move responds to a rapid increase in synthetic tracks and demand for more control over what appears in streaming catalogs.

Labels and provenance metadata are becoming important product infrastructure as platforms decide how to rank, disclose, and moderate machine-generated media.
