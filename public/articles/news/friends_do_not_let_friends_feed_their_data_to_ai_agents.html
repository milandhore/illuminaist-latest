Friends do not let friends feed their data to AI agents

Consumer assistants are encouraging people to share other people’s private information without clear consent.

AI assistants increasingly ask users for context about friends, family, colleagues, and other people who never agreed to share their information. That creates a privacy boundary that standard consent screens often miss.

Product teams need data minimization and permission models that account for third-party information, not just the person who owns the account.
