Amazon Bee launches two personal AI devices

Amazon-owned Bee introduced a clip and a wrist device that turn conversations into notes, tasks, and summaries.

Bee has introduced two personal AI devices designed to capture conversations and transform them into notes, tasks, and summaries. The products connect to Alexa, Amazon services, and common productivity tools.

The low-cost hardware makes ambient assistance more accessible, while also sharpening questions about consent, bystander disclosure, retention, and who controls the resulting data.
