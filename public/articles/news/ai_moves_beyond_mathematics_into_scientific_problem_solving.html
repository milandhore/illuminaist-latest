AI moves beyond mathematics into scientific problem solving

A new study suggests AI systems are improving on theoretical problems in economics, cosmology, probability, and quantum physics.

A study of AI systems working on recent scientific papers found progress beyond mathematics, including probability, economics, cosmology, and quantum physics. The results suggest that models may become useful research partners across more disciplines.

Verification remains the bottleneck: new hypotheses still need reproducible experiments, expert review, and a clear record of how the model reached its result.
