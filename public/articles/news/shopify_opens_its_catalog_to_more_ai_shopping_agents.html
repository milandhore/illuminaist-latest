Shopify opens its catalog to more AI shopping agents

Shopify says AI agents will be able to browse and purchase from a catalog covering more than a billion products.

Shopify is opening its product catalog to more AI agents so shoppers can configure systems to browse and buy on their behalf. The move expands agentic commerce beyond a small set of platform partnerships.

Commerce agents will need identity, consent, payment authorization, and clear records of why a purchase was made before consumers trust them with consequential transactions.
