Nuclear operators look to existing plants for AI power demand

US nuclear operators are upgrading existing reactors as AI data centers drive demand for more dependable low-carbon electricity.

US nuclear operators are pursuing power uprates and modernization projects to increase output from existing reactors. The push is partly driven by rising electricity demand from AI data centers.

The strategy could deliver new capacity faster than building entirely new plants, but it still depends on regulation, grid connections, and the economics of large compute campuses.

Source: MIT Technology Review
