Android adds AI-powered scam detection to Google Messages

Google is expanding message protection with AI that analyzes unknown senders and warns users about likely scams.

Google is adding AI-powered scam detection to Google Messages, using models to identify suspicious patterns in messages from unknown senders. The feature extends existing protections against fraud and impersonation.

The product challenge is balancing timely warnings with privacy: message analysis, false positives, and user appeal paths all need transparent defaults.
