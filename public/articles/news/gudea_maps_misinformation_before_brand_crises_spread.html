Gudea maps misinformation before brand crises spread

Startup Gudea uses AI to track rumors, misinformation, and hate speech across hundreds of platforms for early warning.

Gudea is building an AI platform that maps how rumors, misinformation, and hate speech travel across more than 500 platforms. The company says its context-rich alerts help brands, agencies, and governments respond before a crisis peaks.

The product illustrates a growing market for AI-assisted reputation monitoring, where accuracy and false-positive controls matter as much as speed.
