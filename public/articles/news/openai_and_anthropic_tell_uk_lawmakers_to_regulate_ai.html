OpenAI and Anthropic tell UK lawmakers to regulate AI

OpenAI and Anthropic urged British lawmakers to replace voluntary safety pledges with binding rules and independent testing.

OpenAI and Anthropic told a UK parliamentary committee that voluntary AI safety commitments should be reinforced with binding legislation and external evaluation. Google DeepMind and Meta expressed different views on the regulatory approach.

The hearing reflects a growing divide over how to convert model-safety principles into enforceable obligations for rapidly changing systems.
