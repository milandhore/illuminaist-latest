Trump shares an AI-edited image of White House work

An apparently AI-edited before-and-after image has renewed scrutiny of synthetic media and political communication.

An image shared to promote White House renovation work appeared to contain AI-generated edits and inconsistencies. The episode illustrates how synthetic images can enter political communication without clear disclosure.

Detection tools may help, but provenance standards and visible labeling remain more useful than asking audiences to identify every manipulated image themselves.
