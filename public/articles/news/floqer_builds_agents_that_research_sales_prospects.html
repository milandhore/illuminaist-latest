Floqer builds agents that research sales prospects

Floqer is automating prospect research and CRM updates with agents that continuously collect customer context for sales teams.

Floqer has built an autonomous customer-knowledge platform that researches prospects and updates CRM records. The company says the system can reduce manual work by continuously refreshing context for sales teams.

Reliable enterprise agents need source attribution, approval workflows, and a clear way for operators to correct inaccurate or stale customer data.
