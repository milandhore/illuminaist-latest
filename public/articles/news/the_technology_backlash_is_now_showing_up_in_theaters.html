The technology backlash is now showing up in theaters

A new wave of films is examining the power, influence, and social costs of Silicon Valley and artificial intelligence.

Several 2026 films are turning their attention to technology leaders, AI, and the cultural power of Silicon Valley. The stories reflect a shift from treating the industry as inevitable progress to examining its consequences.

For builders, the cultural response is a reminder that product adoption is shaped by public trust and narrative as much as by benchmark scores.
