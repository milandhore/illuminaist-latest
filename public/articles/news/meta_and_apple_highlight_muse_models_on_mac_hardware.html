Meta and Apple highlight Muse models on Mac hardware

Meta says its Muse Spark Open model can run on high-end Mac systems through Apple’s MLX framework and large unified memory.

Meta says Muse Spark Open can run on desktop Macs using multiple Apple M5 Ultra systems and the MLX framework. The announcement highlights how open-weight models are being matched with consumer hardware for local inference.

Running capable models locally can reduce cloud dependence, but memory requirements and energy use remain significant for most teams.
