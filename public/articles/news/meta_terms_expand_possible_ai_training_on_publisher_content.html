Meta terms expand possible AI training on publisher content

Meta’s updated commercial terms may permit crawling linked sites for AI training, prompting questions about consent and compensation.

Meta’s updated commercial terms are set to cover content on sites linked to its advertisers, including news publishers, for possible use in developing AI systems. Legal analysts say the language could weaken common blocking signals such as robots.txt.

Publishers and advertisers will need to review the terms closely and clarify how content permissions, attribution, and compensation work in AI training pipelines.

Source: The Verge
