Flock privacy debate broadens the surveillance backlash

Critics say AI-enabled camera and audio systems can expand surveillance beyond their advertised use, increasing pressure for transparency.

The debate around Flock Safety is growing as critics challenge how private surveillance systems describe their capabilities. The controversy includes concerns about audio collection, license-plate data, government contracts, and the limits of vendor claims.

For AI surveillance buyers, the episode reinforces the need for narrow purpose definitions, independent testing, retention limits, and public accountability.
