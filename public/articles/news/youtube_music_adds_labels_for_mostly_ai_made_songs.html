YouTube Music adds labels for mostly AI-made songs

YouTube Music is introducing labels for tracks created primarily with generative AI as synthetic music floods streaming services.

YouTube Music is rolling out labels identifying songs created primarily with generative AI. The move follows a sharp increase in synthetic music uploads and similar transparency efforts from other streaming platforms.

Labels can help listeners make informed choices, but the industry still needs consistent definitions for partial AI use, voice cloning, and how detection decisions can be challenged.
