AI labs are buying data from failed startups

A new market is brokering code, emails, and workspace archives from closed startups as AI labs seek richer training data.

Companies are beginning to broker data from defunct startups, including code, emails, and workspace archives, to AI labs building training and reinforcement-learning environments. The market reflects growing pressure to find high-quality real-world data.

The practice raises difficult questions about employee privacy, customer confidentiality, intellectual property, and whether old data can be repurposed under the original terms of collection.

Source: MIT Technology Review
