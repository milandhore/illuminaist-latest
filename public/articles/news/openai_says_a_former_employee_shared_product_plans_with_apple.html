OpenAI says a former employee shared product plans with Apple

A trade-secret dispute alleges that confidential OpenAI product files were shown to Apple before a former employee changed companies.

OpenAI alleges that a former employee took confidential files about product plans and presented them to Apple executives before joining the company. The dispute highlights the sensitivity of model roadmaps, supplier information, and unreleased hardware work.

Strong access logging, data classification, and offboarding controls are becoming core engineering practices for AI companies.
