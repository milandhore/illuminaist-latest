Google launches an AI-powered Universal Cart

Google’s new US shopping tool brings items from multiple retailers into one AI-assisted basket with comparison and price alerts.

Google has launched Universal Cart in the United States, allowing shoppers to collect products from multiple retailers through Gemini and Google Search. The tool supports comparison and price-drop alerts in one shopping flow.

The feature is another step toward AI agents acting across commerce systems, making permissions, ranking transparency, and checkout security important design concerns.
