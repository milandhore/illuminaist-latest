Amazon’s ambient AI strategy aims to reduce app switching

Amazon devices chief Panos Panay says future wearables could work quietly in the background and anticipate tasks across a user’s day.

Amazon devices chief Panos Panay described a shift toward ambient AI that operates in the background instead of requiring users to open separate apps. New Bee wearables capture conversations, create summaries, and connect to productivity tools.

The strategy could make assistants more useful, but it also makes consent, recording controls, encryption, and deletion policies central to product trust.
