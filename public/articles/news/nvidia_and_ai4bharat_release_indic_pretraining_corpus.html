NVIDIA and AI4Bharat release Indic pretraining corpus

A new 2.8-trillion-token corpus covers 22 Indian languages and includes an open pipeline for generating and checking multilingual data.

NVIDIA and AI4Bharat have released Nemotron-pretraining-Indic-v1, a 2.8-trillion-token corpus covering 22 Indian languages. The package also includes a GPU-accelerated pipeline for generating and quality-checking synthetic multilingual data.

The release could lower the data barrier for Indic-language models while giving researchers a more transparent base for evaluation and adaptation.
