Laplace Intelligence red-teams robots before deployment

Laplace uses high-fidelity simulation and AI agents to generate edge cases for robots, autonomous vehicles, and physical AI systems.

Laplace Intelligence is building simulations that generate difficult scenarios for robots, autonomous vehicles, and other physical AI systems. Its agents are intended to find failures before hardware reaches the real world.

Simulation-based red teaming can make safety testing more systematic, especially when teams preserve scenario coverage and connect virtual failures to physical validation.
