A robot pendant review exposes the limits of AI companions

A review of the Friend AI pendant finds limited personalization and intrusive behavior despite its conversational ambitions.

A review of the Friend AI pendant found that the device can speak out of turn and offer repetitive commentary despite its promise of a more personal assistant. The experience exposes the gap between conversational novelty and useful companionship.

Wearable AI products need a strong interaction model: when to listen, when to stay silent, and how a user can inspect or delete the system’s memory.
