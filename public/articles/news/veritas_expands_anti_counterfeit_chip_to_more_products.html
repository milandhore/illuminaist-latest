Veritas expands anti-counterfeit chip to more products

Veritas has launched a chip for jewelry, trophies, and fragrance bottles that lets buyers verify authenticity with a secure tap.

Veritas has launched a second-generation anti-counterfeit chip for hard-surface goods such as jewelry, trophies, and fragrance bottles. A tap-and-read response lets brands and buyers check an item’s identity through a secure system.

The product shows how AI-adjacent identity infrastructure is moving beyond software into physical supply chains and consumer goods.
