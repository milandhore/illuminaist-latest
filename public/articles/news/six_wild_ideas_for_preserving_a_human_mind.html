Six wild ideas for preserving a human mind

Researchers and futurists are exploring brain freezing, memory uploading, and other speculative paths to preserve identity.

New research and long-running experiments are renewing debate about whether personal identity could be preserved beyond biological death. Proposals range from improved cryonics to memory capture and brain-computer interfaces.

Most approaches remain speculative, but the discussion connects directly to current questions about neural data, digital replicas, and what it would mean for an AI system to model a person.
