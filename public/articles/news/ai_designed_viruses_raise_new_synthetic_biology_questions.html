AI-designed viruses raise new synthetic-biology questions

Researchers are using generative AI to draft viral blueprints, opening a new debate about capability, oversight, and biosecurity.

Researchers are beginning to use generative AI to draft genetic blueprints for microscopic viruses. The work is still an early research step, but it shows how quickly AI is moving from analyzing biology to helping design it.

The development increases the need for screening, controlled access, and clear publication standards as synthetic biology tools become more capable.

Source: MIT Technology Review
