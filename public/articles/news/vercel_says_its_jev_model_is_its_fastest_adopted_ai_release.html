Vercel says its Jev model is its fastest-adopted AI release

Vercel reports that 35% of paid teams used its new decision model within two weeks, pointing to demand for focused AI tools.

Vercel says its Jev decision model reached 35% adoption among paid teams within two weeks, nearly twice the rate of its next most popular model. The company positions the model as lightweight and cost-effective for specialized tasks.

The adoption claim reflects a broader shift toward smaller, focused models that can be deployed quickly rather than one general model for every workflow.
