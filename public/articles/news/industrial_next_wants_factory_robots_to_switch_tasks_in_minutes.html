Industrial Next wants factory robots to switch tasks in minutes

Industrial Next is using foundation models for vision and motion to retool manufacturing robots faster than traditional programming allows.

Industrial Next demonstrated robotic stations that can retool a production line in minutes instead of weeks. Its systems combine vision, context, and motion models to adapt manipulation tasks.

Faster reconfiguration could help manufacturers handle more product variation, but reliability, worker safety, and validation at the edge of a live factory remain critical constraints.
