Retinal implant helps restore functional sight

A camera-linked retinal implant helped most trial users with advanced macular degeneration read and use smartphones after training.

A retinal implant system called Prima combines camera-equipped glasses with a small implant that substitutes for damaged photoreceptors. In European trials, more than 80% of users could read, solve crosswords, and use smartphones after training.

The system still awaits broader regulatory clearance, but it points to a practical role for computer vision and neural interfaces in restoring lost function.

Source: MIT Technology Review
