Scimplify raises $25M for AI-designed specialty materials

The Indian startup plans to use AI to speed the design and production of chemicals and critical materials for semiconductor and energy supply chains.

Indian startup Scimplify has raised $25 million for an AI-driven platform that helps design and produce specialty chemicals and critical materials. The company is targeting semiconductor, defense, and energy supply chains.

AI-assisted materials discovery could reduce iteration time, but industrial value will depend on laboratory validation, repeatable manufacturing, and reliable sourcing at scale.
