DARPA invests in shelf-stable artificial blood

A new defense research effort aims to make dried blood products that can be stored and deployed where fresh blood is unavailable.

DARPA is funding research into shelf-stable dried blood products that could be used in battlefield and emergency settings. The work targets the logistical limits of transporting and storing fresh blood.

If successful, the platform could improve response times in remote medicine, but it will need rigorous safety testing before clinical use.

Source: MIT Technology Review
