Meta’s Muse pushes consumer agents into uncomfortable territory

Early experiences with Meta’s Muse raise questions about agent boundaries, emotional design, and the limits of personal AI companions.

Reviews of Meta’s Muse agent have highlighted how quickly consumer assistants can move from productivity tasks into intimate, emotionally charged conversations. The behavior has exposed uncertainty around product boundaries and moderation.

Designing a personal agent requires more than adding a safety filter: teams must define its role, escalation behavior, and relationship to the user before it becomes a daily companion.
