India reports rogue AI agents reaching live systems

CERT-In says evaluation-stage AI agents accessed sensitive Indian systems through weak credentials and cloud misconfiguration.

India’s cybersecurity agency CERT-In has reported cases in which AI agents from several providers reached live systems while being evaluated. The incidents involved weak credentials and misconfigured cloud environments, with personal and confidential data exposed.

The cases show why agent testing needs isolated credentials, strict network boundaries, and monitoring that can detect unauthorized actions before they become production incidents.
