Apertus 1.5 adds multimodal and reasoning capabilities

The open-source Apertus update adds image and audio processing, a thinking mode, and a 262k-token context window under Apache 2.0.

The open-source Apertus team has released version 1.5 with native image and audio processing, a new reasoning mode, and a fourfold context increase to 262,000 tokens. The 8B and 70B weights are available under Apache 2.0.

The update gives developers a permissively licensed multimodal option with tool-use capabilities and enough context for larger documents and codebases.
