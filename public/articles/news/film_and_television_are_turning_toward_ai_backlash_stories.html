Film and television are turning toward AI backlash stories

A new wave of films about tech power and AI reflects growing cultural unease with Silicon Valley’s influence.

Several 2026 films and documentaries are examining technology leaders, AI influence, and the consequences of concentrated digital power. The trend reflects a broader cultural response to the promises and failures of the current AI boom.

Media narratives can shape public expectations, which makes honest communication about capability, limits, and accountability increasingly important for AI companies.
