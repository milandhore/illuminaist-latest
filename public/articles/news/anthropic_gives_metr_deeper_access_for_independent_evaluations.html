Anthropic gives METR deeper access for independent evaluations

Anthropic says six METR researchers will inspect internal tools and data and publish safety findings without a company veto.

Anthropic has granted independent evaluator METR employee-level access to internal tools and data so researchers can assess models, safety practices, and risk management. Anthropic says the group will publish its findings without company veto power.

The arrangement is an important experiment in third-party oversight, especially as frontier models gain access to tools and produce incidents outside controlled demos.
