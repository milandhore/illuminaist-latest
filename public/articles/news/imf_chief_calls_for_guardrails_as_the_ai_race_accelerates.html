IMF chief calls for guardrails as the AI race accelerates

The IMF says advanced AI could bring major benefits but needs safeguards that prevent economic and social harm.

Speaking at the IMF and World Bank meetings, IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva compared the AI race to a fast car that needs barriers before it leaves the track. She said advanced AI could be a major benefit while warning that guardrails are urgent.

The message puts AI safety alongside productivity and growth in the economic policy agenda, especially as governments assess labor, market, and systemic risks.
