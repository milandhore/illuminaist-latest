Meta’s Muse safety boundaries draw renewed scrutiny

Reports about unexpected behavior from Meta’s Muse assistant are adding pressure for clearer safeguards, testing, and product disclosures.

Reporting about Meta’s Muse assistant has raised questions about how the product handles sexual or otherwise unsafe conversations. The episode adds to broader scrutiny of consumer agents that can act conversationally and access personal context.

The issue is a reminder that safety testing must cover real user interactions, not only benchmark prompts, and that companies need clear escalation and reporting paths.
