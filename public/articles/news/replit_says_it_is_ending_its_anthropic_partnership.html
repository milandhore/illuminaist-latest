Replit says it is ending its Anthropic partnership

Replit’s CEO cited cost, performance, and strategy while describing a shift toward OpenAI models for the platform.

Replit CEO Amjad Masad said the platform is ending its partnership with Anthropic and shifting more attention toward OpenAI models. He pointed to cost, performance, and strategic priorities, while reporting noted that the change is disputed by insiders.

The move illustrates how AI application companies are balancing model quality, safety infrastructure, pricing, and supplier concentration.
