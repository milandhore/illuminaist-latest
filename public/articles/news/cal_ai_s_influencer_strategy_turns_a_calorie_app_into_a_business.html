Cal AI’s influencer strategy turns a calorie app into a business

The founder of Cal AI says a network of micro-influencers helped grow the app to a $30 million business before its acquisition.

Cal AI founder Zach Yadegari described how paid micro-influencers helped grow the AI calorie-tracking app into a $30 million business before its acquisition by MyFitnessPal. He is now building another AI assistant startup.

The story shows that distribution and trust can matter as much as model capability when consumer AI products compete for attention.
