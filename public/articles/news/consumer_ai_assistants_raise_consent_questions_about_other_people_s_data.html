Consumer AI assistants raise consent questions about other people’s data

The spread of personal AI assistants is encouraging users to share friends’ and colleagues’ information without explicit consent.

Personal AI assistants make it easy to capture and summarize conversations, but they can also ingest information about friends, family, coworkers, and customers who never agreed to be processed.

Product teams need consent-aware defaults, clear recording indicators, retention limits, and ways for affected people to request correction or deletion.
