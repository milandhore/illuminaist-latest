Mark Wahlberg says film AI should support, not replace, creators

The actor and director supports AI for production tasks but says storytelling and real actors should remain central to filmmaking.

Mark Wahlberg told TechCrunch Disrupt that AI can help with production work such as improving stadium visuals, but should not replace storytelling or real actors. He also said he would consider a future AI replica of his likeness for legacy projects.

The comments reflect the entertainment industry’s emerging compromise: use AI to extend production capability while setting boundaries around consent, identity, and creative credit.
