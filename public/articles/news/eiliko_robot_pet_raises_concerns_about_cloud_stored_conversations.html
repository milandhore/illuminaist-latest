Eiliko robot pet raises concerns about cloud-stored conversations

A new AI robot pet reportedly records conversations and stores data on Chinese servers, prompting privacy and security questions.

The AI-powered Eiliko robot pet records user conversations and stores data on servers in China, according to reporting. Security researchers and consumer advocates have raised concerns about access controls and potential compromise.

Connected toys show why privacy reviews must cover the full data path—from microphones and mobile apps to cloud storage and third-party analytics.
