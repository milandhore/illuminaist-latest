AI agents are coming to Rivian vehicles

Rivian plans an in-car assistant and future integrations with third-party agents while keeping safety-critical controls separate.

Rivian has unveiled a voice assistant for its electric vehicles and plans to support third-party AI agents from companies including Anthropic, OpenAI, and Meta. The automaker says safety-critical controls will remain separate from general-purpose assistants.

The architecture matters because useful in-car agents need broad context without being able to make unsafe changes to driving systems or vehicle controls.
