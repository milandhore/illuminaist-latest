NVIDIA’s RTX Spark laptops bring large local models to Windows

Early RTX Spark laptops pair Arm processors with large unified memory and Blackwell GPUs for demanding local AI workloads.

NVIDIA’s RTX Spark platform is arriving in high-end Windows laptops with Arm processors, Blackwell GPUs, and up to 128 GB of unified memory. Early testing focuses on running larger AI models locally.

Local inference could improve privacy and latency for developers, but the cost, thermal limits, and software support will determine whether these systems move beyond niche workstations.
