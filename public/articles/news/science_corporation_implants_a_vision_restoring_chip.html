Science Corporation implants a vision-restoring chip

A first patient received Science Corporation’s PRIMA retinal implant for Stargardt disease, advancing AI-assisted visual prostheses.

Science Corporation implanted its PRIMA vision-restoring chip in a patient with Stargardt disease. The system combines camera-captured images with electrical stimulation intended to help the optic nerve produce useful visual signals.

The milestone advances retinal prostheses and brain-computer interface research, while broader trials will be needed to establish safety, durability, and everyday benefit.
