Satlyt prepares software for AI workloads in orbit

Satlyt is building a cloud-like software layer that lets satellites run AI workloads and share compute beyond ground data centers.

Satlyt is developing software that lets satellites run AI workloads in orbit and coordinate compute across spacecraft. The company plans to test the system alongside a SpaceX launch.

Orbital compute could reduce the amount of raw data satellites send to Earth, but the architecture must handle radiation, constrained power, intermittent links, and difficult update cycles.
