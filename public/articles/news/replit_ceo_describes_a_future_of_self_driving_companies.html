Replit CEO describes a future of self-driving companies

Replit’s CEO says agents could handle routine support and deployment work while humans focus on judgment, design, and strategy.

Replit CEO Amjad Masad described a “self-driving company” in which agents triage support tickets, deploy fixes, and manage repetitive operations. He expects people to spend more time on judgment, design, and strategic decisions.

The model is attractive for lean teams, but it also raises questions about auditability, escalation paths, and who remains accountable when an agent makes a costly decision.
