AI-generated avatars intensify creator-content theft

Comedians say synthetic avatars are copying their jokes and outperforming original clips, exposing gaps in platform safeguards.

Comedians are reporting that AI-generated, sexualized avatars are reusing their jokes and republishing them with polished production. The synthetic clips can outperform the originals on social platforms, turning creator work into raw material for automated content businesses.

The dispute highlights the need for better provenance tools, faster takedowns, and clearer rules for training and monetizing AI-generated media.

Source: Wired
