Anthropic warns small models could spread through IoT devices

Anthropic says compact models may eventually enable self-replicating malware that moves across connected household devices.

Anthropic has warned that increasingly capable small models could help create self-replicating malware that spreads through connected devices. The company says current technical barriers are significant, but the direction is worth preparing for.

The security response includes stronger device isolation, signed model and software updates, least-privilege credentials, and monitoring for unusual autonomous behavior.
