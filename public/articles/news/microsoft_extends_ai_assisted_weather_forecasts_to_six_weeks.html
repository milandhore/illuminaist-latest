Microsoft extends AI-assisted weather forecasts to six weeks

Microsoft researchers say a new machine-learning tool can improve reliable forecasts beyond the usual two-week horizon.

Microsoft researchers have introduced an open machine-learning tool designed to extend useful weather forecasts from about two weeks to as much as six weeks. The work is aimed at planning for agriculture, emergency response, and infrastructure.

Longer reliable forecasts could be valuable for operational decisions, but adoption will depend on independent validation across regions and extreme-weather conditions.
