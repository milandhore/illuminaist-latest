Google and Meta reignite the frontier-model race

New model releases from Google and Meta have shifted attention back toward competition at the top of the AI market.

Google and Meta have announced new frontier-model releases that they say can compete with leading systems from OpenAI and Anthropic. The launches show how quickly the rankings and capabilities at the top of the market can change.

For developers, model choice is becoming a portfolio decision involving latency, cost, deployment control, and evaluation fit rather than a single leaderboard position.
